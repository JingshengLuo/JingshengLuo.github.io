Qi Xiang, Zhengqian Jin, and Teng Deng contributed equally to this work.

Nitrogen–boron co-doped carbon nanofiber interlayers combine polar anchoring sites with a conductive, wettable network to confine iodine redox intermediates near the cathode and reduce polyiodide crosstalk with the zinc anode in aqueous Zn–I₂ batteries.
