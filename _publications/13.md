![Three-stage framework for digital research](/images/viz-matter-102777.jpg)

Publication volume in materials and energy research has outrun the reading capacity of any individual group. This piece proposes a three-stage framework — collecting literature, processing information, integrating notes — and argues that the bottleneck is no longer access but the absence of a workflow that survives the volume. Related work on the metadata layer is collected under [Writing](/year-archive/).
