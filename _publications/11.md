Intramolecular polarisation is used to reshape the Li<sup>+</sup> solvation shell so that desolvation stays facile at low temperature, while the derived interphase keeps an NCM811 cathode structurally intact under high-voltage operation. The cell operates at &minus;30&nbsp;°C and up to 4.9&nbsp;V.
